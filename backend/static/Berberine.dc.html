Common questions

What people ask before their first bottle.

When should I take it?

With your largest meal of the day. Splitting the serving across two meals is also common practice.

Can I take it with TriFiber Daily?

Yes — that's the intended routine. Fiber in the morning, berberine with your main meal.

Is this a weight-loss product?

No. It is a food supplement positioned around everyday metabolic wellness, and it is not a substitute for prescription treatment.
