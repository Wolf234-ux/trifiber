How to use

One scoop. Cold water. Morning.

Fiber works on frequency, not intensity. The routine matters more than the dose, which is why taste and texture were engineered first.

01

Stir into 250 ml water

Cold or room temperature. Lemon-Jeera is savoury-citrus, not sweet.

02

Drink it within a few minutes

Soluble fiber thickens as it sits, so fresh is better.

03

Build up over the first week

Start with half a scoop if you're new to added fiber, and keep water intake up.
