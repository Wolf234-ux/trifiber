# TriFiber Health — Context & Business Strategy Document

## Executive Overview
**TriFiber Health** is building the everyday metabolic nutrition platform for the post-GLP-1 world. By bridging the gap between low-compliance dietary supplements and high-barrier prescription GLP-1 medications, TriFiber Health delivers evidence-led, daily preventive nutrition.

```
       [ Generic Supplements ]  --->  [ TRIFIBER HEALTH ]  <---  [ Prescription GLP-1s ]
     (Low adherence, weak claims)     (Daily habit, science-led)    (High cost, side effects)
```

---

## 1. Core Problem & Market Opportunity

### The Modern Diet Has Created Two Connected Gaps:
1. **The Fiber Gap**:
   - Refined carbohydrates, processed foods, and low plant diversity leave >90% of adults under-fibered.
   - Symptoms: Irregularity, poor satiety, frequent snacking, digestive discomfort.
   - Traditional market flaw: Fiber products historically targeted occasional constipation rather than daily foundational nutrition.

2. **The Metabolic Gap**:
   - The global rise of GLP-1 receptor agonists (e.g., Semaglutide/Ozempic) has transformed public understanding of satiety, post-meal glucose spikes, insulin, and appetite.
   - Millions of consumers seek glucose stability and appetite control but do not qualify for or desire high-cost prescription drugs.

## 3. Market Size & Regulatory Positioning

### Market Drivers:
- **Fiber Supplements**: Scaling to $25.6B by 2033 (from $15.5B in 2025).
- **Digestive Health**: Projected to hit $36.2B by 2033 (11.3% CAGR).
- **Weight Loss / Metabolic Supplements**: Reaching $71.6B by 2030.
- **India Nutraceuticals**: Growing to $55–57B by 2030.

### Regulatory Claims Strategy ("Prove More, Claim Less"):
- **India (FSSAI)**: Schedule IV (Botanicals) for Berberine HCl; Schedule VII (Probiotics/Prebiotics) for TriFiber.
- **United States (FDA)**: Structure/Function claims only under DSHEA.
- **European Union (EFSA)**: Regulatory claims under Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006.
