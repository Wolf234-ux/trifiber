# TriFiber Health — Master Implementation Plan (plan.md)

## Strategic Objectives
Build and launch a high-performance, evidence-led D2C e-commerce platform and digital health experience for TriFiber Health using a **Python (FastAPI)** backend and a **React (Vite)** frontend.

---

## 1. Roadmap & Key Milestones (18-Month Master Plan)

```
[0-3 Months] Phase 1: Foundation
  ├── Regulatory mapping (FSSAI, FDA, EFSA)
  ├── Scientific advisory board assembly
  └── Initial formulation prototypes & D2C UX design

[3-6 Months] Phase 2: Build & Lab Testing
  ├── Stability & sensory testing (Lemon-Jeera & Citrus)
  ├── Full-stack D2C platform build (FastAPI + React)
  └── Brand identity, packaging & QR quality tracking engine

[6-9 Months] Phase 3: Validation & Pilot Manufacturing
  ├── Lock commercial formulations
  ├── Pilot manufacturing batch production
  └── Clinical feasibility pilot study initiation

[9-12 Months] Phase 4: Controlled Launch
  ├── India D2C store go-live
  ├── Lead acquisition & subscription engine launch
  └── Direct consumer feedback loops & retention optimization

[12-18 Months] Phase 5: Scale & Global Expansion
  ├── Market expansion: UAE, US, UK, GCC
  ├── Clinical readout publication
  └── Series A fundraising prepare ($3M–$5M)
```

## 3. Product & Technology Deliverables

1. **D2C E-Commerce Storefront (React + Vite)**:
   - Product detail pages for **TriFiber Daily** and **Berberine Metabolic Balance**.
   - Interactive metabolic quiz for customized product bundles.
   - Batch lookup modal linked to QR codes for Certificate of Analysis (COA).
   - Frictionless cart, subscription management, and checkout simulator.

2. **Backend API (Python FastAPI)**:
   - Dynamic product management & pricing per currency (INR, USD, AED).
   - QR Batch Verification API returning batch-level test results.
   - Quiz evaluation algorithm generating metabolic scores.
   - Regulatory claims verification service.
   - Auto-Marketing Campaign Generator (AI copy hooks & content strategy).

3. **Auto-Marketing Engine**:
   - Automated lead capture funnel.
   - Social proof & habit-tracking loop.
   - Influencer & healthcare practitioner referral integration.
